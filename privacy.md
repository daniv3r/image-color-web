Flow Master: Image Coloring is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

## Information We Collect

**Photos you choose to convert** — when you pick a photo to turn into a paint-by-numbers canvas, it is uploaded temporarily to our server so we can generate your painting.

**Painting data** — the resulting painting (its numbered regions and color palette) is stored so you can resume and revisit it later. This is derived data, not the original photo.

**Device identifier** — the app identifies your device with a random ID generated on your phone. It is not tied to your name, Apple ID, or email address, and we don't require an account.

**Diagnostic logs** — the app keeps local diagnostic logs on your device (such as which screens were opened or errors encountered) to help us troubleshoot problems if you contact support. These logs stay on your device unless you choose to share them with us, and never include your photos or paintings.

**Purchase records** — when you buy credits, the purchase is processed by the App Store and RevenueCat, our subscription and purchase management provider.

## How We Use Your Information

- To generate your paint-by-numbers painting from a photo you choose
- To let you resume and manage your paintings across sessions
- To process credit purchases
- To diagnose problems and improve the app

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## Photo Handling

Your original photo is uploaded only to generate your painting and is deleted from our servers as soon as conversion completes. If a conversion fails, the original may be retained briefly until it's cleared by our automated cleanup process. We do not view, retain, or use your photos to train any model or for any purpose beyond generating your painting.

## Third-Party Services

Flow Master: Image Coloring uses the following third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

- **RevenueCat** — to process and manage in-app credit purchases
- **Apple** — for App Store transactions and distribution, and for crash reports if you opt in via your device's iOS settings

## Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

## Your Rights

Because the app doesn't use accounts, we can't automatically look up or delete your data by email or login. If you'd like your photos, paintings, or device data deleted, contact us at the support address below with your device details and we'll process the request manually.

## Children's Privacy

Flow Master: Image Coloring is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us via the [Support](support.html) page.
